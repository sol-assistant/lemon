Drag a box around the words you want to change. The old words get erased by the AI eraser, then new text appears in the same spot, matched to the font, size, color and angle. Type your words and you’re done.

Tips: one line at a time works best. Box just the words, with a little room around them. Pinch to zoom in for small text. Change the font or color in the next step if the match is off.

The AI eraser downloads once (about 30 MB), then it’s saved on this device and works offline. Unlimited.
